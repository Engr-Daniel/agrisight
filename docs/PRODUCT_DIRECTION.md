# Product identity and stakeholder purpose

Owner clarification recorded 2026-10-30 from the supplied rationale discussion. This governs future product communication. The proposed navigation is a target, not an implemented redesign or evidence of user interviews.

## Purpose

The owner is learning to execute a real-world agricultural analytics engagement professionally. That learning purpose belongs to development and repository documentation. The stakeholder deliverable is **AgriSight: Agricultural Data Intelligence and Decision Support**.

An engagement starts with a stakeholder's agricultural question. The analyst obtains appropriate authorized data, understands its structure and limits, investigates the question, develops defensible indicators and communicates findings and supported implications. The website presents that analytical outcome. Its identity should not be the developer's learning journey, portfolio milestones or software experiments.

## Product and repository responsibilities

The website communicates agricultural questions, findings, visualizations, interpretation, supported recommendations, data coverage/source/reference period and relevant limitations. About AgriSight explains purpose, analytical scope and sources. Personal learning objectives, M0–M6, test totals, dependency details and synthetic engineering benchmarks should not lead the stakeholder experience.

The repository retains learning context, protocols, SQL/Python reconciliation, tests, reproducibility and detailed methods. Preserve the backend and engineering work. Its value does not require prominent agricultural product navigation.

This supersedes the earlier two-layer approach **as the intended primary website structure**. Existing Engineering studies and Evidence & methods pages remain current software until an explicitly implemented refactor; recording this decision does not remove them. Technical detail and synthetic exploration should move out of the primary stakeholder experience and remain available in repository documentation.

Pages must earn their content. Distinguish observed survey missingness, unresolved measurement lineage and synthetic defect experiments. Recommendations must follow a stated finding and decision context; do not invent programme outcomes or causal/policy prescriptions. Do not create empty pages simply to match this menu.

## Integrity and analytical depth

Keep material interpretation facts beside findings in concise language. Crop participation is an unweighted share of valid household responses in the released sample, with source, reference period and denominator. It is not prevalence among all Nigerian farmers. Plot-area missingness bounds do not establish measurement accuracy or national farm size. These contextual notes belong in professional stakeholder communication.

Synthetic matching/workload results are engineering evidence, not actual programme performance. They belong in the repository unless a clearly stated stakeholder decision warrants a separately labelled feature. Do not use engineering metrics to compensate for limited agricultural findings or combine survey and synthetic populations.

Current agricultural evidence consists of crop participation and the plot-area missingness study. Additional content requires justified agricultural questions, feasibility checks, analysis and validation. Attractive presentation must not imply broader results already exist.

Removing developer-facing copy does not authorize fabricated clients, operational enrollment, deployment, live services, measured impact or national inference. Source-data permissions and truthful scope remain mandatory. Recording this direction does not authorize a backend rebuild, new framework, new experiment or deployment.
