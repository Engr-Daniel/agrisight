# Publication-copy handoff

The independently read saved NBS access conditions require an electronic copy of
reports/publications based on the requested data to be sent to the National Data
Archive. This file prepares that owner follow-up; it does not record a submission.

- Publication: AgriSight agricultural intelligence website and supporting analyses.
- Demo: https://agrisight-demo.streamlit.app/
- Repository: https://github.com/Engr-Daniel/agrisight
- Source: National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), Nigeria. Nigeria - National
  Agricultural Sample Survey 2023 (NGA-NBS-NASS-2023-V01). NBS Microdata Catalogue.
- Results: historical unweighted sample aggregates with explicit units, periods,
  denominators, exclusions and limitations. No microdata or respondent locations
  accompany the public website.
- Supporting material: `reports/` findings and `docs/PUBLIC_HOSTING_REVIEW.md`.

Use the archive's verified contact route from the owner's authenticated portal;
no recipient address is inferred here. Record the submission date and receipt in
private project records. The owner can then check the corresponding TASK item.
**Status: prepared; not sent.**
